Questions

Is it a medical device?

No. Readiva is a record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your health.

Where is my data?

On your phone. There is no account and no server that receives your readings.

What if I lose my phone?

Your diary is safe if you have a backup. Make an encrypted backup in the app (it is free on every plan), then save both the backup file and your Recovery Key outside your phone, for example in a cloud drive or on another device. If they stay only on the phone, they are lost with it. On a new phone, restore from the backup using your key. If you have no backup, we cannot recover your readings, because they were never sent to us.

Is it free?

Yes, there is a free plan. Paid plans add more profiles, custom parameters, full history and reports.
